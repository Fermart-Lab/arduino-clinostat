# Research and media guide

## Article

Use the [published article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69037-4) as the scientific reference. [Figure 3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69037-4#Fig3) shows the manufacturing application; [Methods → Construction of clinostat](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69037-4#Sec17) describes the apparatus. Bibliographic metadata are in [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff).

The article credits Javier G. Fernandez with designing and building the clinostat and acknowledges Sarah Wasifa Ferdousi’s help with its construction.
